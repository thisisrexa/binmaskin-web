# Privacy Policy

## Scope

This notice describes the contact information submitted through this website and
the published choices for cookies and analytics. It concerns this website. The
companies featured here are legally independent; a separate engagement with a
company may involve additional information and its own notices.

## Information in an enquiry

The contact form collects your name, email address, selected topic and message.
A phone number is optional. The information you send is delivered to the inbox
associated with info@binmaskin.solutions so your enquiry can be reviewed and
answered.

Include only the information needed to explain your request. Do not submit
passwords, one-time codes, complete payment-card details or identity documents
through the form. Confirm an appropriate channel before sending documents needed
for a particular engagement.

## Contact channels and website preferences

You can also contact the published email address or telephone number directly.
Information you choose to include in those communications becomes part of the
enquiry. If you use an external email, telephone or messaging service, that
service’s own privacy terms also apply to your use of it.

The Cookie Notice explains the website’s published language and consent
settings. Open Cookie settings in the footer to review the optional analytics
choice.

## Requests about your information

Contact info@binmaskin.solutions to ask about the information associated with
your enquiry, its use, recipients or retention, or to request access,
correction, deletion or restriction where applicable under the relevant
data-protection law. Explain the request without including unnecessary sensitive
information. The handling of a request may depend on its circumstances and
applicable legal obligations.

## Contact

For questions about this notice or information submitted through the website,
email info@binmaskin.solutions.
