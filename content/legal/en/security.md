# Security and Fraud Prevention

## Check unexpected requests

Use this page to check messages that claim to represent BinMaskin or a company
featured on this website. If a request involves money, confidential information
or access to a device, pause and verify it independently before acting.

Open this website directly and use its published contact details. Do not rely
only on a number or link included in an unexpected message. Check the sender’s
full email address and website domain; a familiar name or logo alone does not
establish authenticity.

## Verify payments independently

Before making a payment, confirm the legal company receiving it, the reason for
the payment and the relevant written agreement or invoice. Independently verify
any unexpected change to bank details, payment method or recipient using a
contact channel you have already established as genuine.

Do not let urgency or a request for secrecy replace these checks.

## Protect confidential information

Do not share passwords, one-time verification codes, bank PINs or complete
payment-card details in response to an unsolicited message. Do not install
software or grant access to your device at the direction of an unverified
sender.

If documents or technical access are needed for a legitimate engagement, confirm
the request, its purpose and the appropriate channel directly with the
responsible company first.

## Report a suspicious message

Contact info@binmaskin.solutions or +971 55 612 0279 using the details on this
website. Provide a short description, the sender’s address and the date of the
message. Remove passwords, verification codes, payment-card details and
unrelated personal information from anything you share.

If money has already been sent or an account may be compromised, contact the
relevant bank or service provider through its official channels and report
suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.

## Scope of this guidance

This page provides practical verification advice. It is not a guarantee that a
message, transaction or system is secure, and it does not authorize testing or
accessing any system.
