# Cookie Notice

## Website settings

The website’s cookie controls describe two essential settings: the selected
language and the saved cookie choice. They are shown separately from optional
analytics.

## Language and saved choice

NEXT_LOCALE records the selected website language, English or Arabic. The
consent setting, bm-consent, records the cookie choice; the settings panel
describes this choice as lasting one year.

Blocking or deleting browser storage may reset saved preferences. Essential
settings are marked as always on in the website’s controls.

## Optional analytics

The settings panel currently identifies Google Analytics as optional and not
active on this site yet. It states that analytics is not loaded before opt-in.

## Review your choice

Open Cookie settings in the footer. Choose Reject all to select essential
settings only, or review the analytics switch and use Save choice. Accept all
selects the optional choice as well. You can return to the settings later to
review or change the preference recorded by this browser.

Browser settings can also be used to block or remove cookies. For questions
about the website’s cookie choices, contact info@binmaskin.solutions.
