Prime Advanced General Trading connects businesses with products, materials, and
commercial opportunities across more than one sector.

## The trade

- Products and materials for projects that already have a specification
- Commercial paths between suppliers and the desk that needs them
- A wider trading surface than a single category allows

The company sits with the group so a materials or project need can be answered
without starting from a stranger.
