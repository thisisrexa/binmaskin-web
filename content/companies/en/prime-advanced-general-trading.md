Prime Advanced General Trading supports exhibition and event work, helping
businesses turn their requirements into a clear presence in a physical space.
The focus is on what needs to be presented, how the space will be used and what
the event needs to achieve.

Exhibition stands and event settings bring design, materials and on-site
coordination together. A clear brief helps connect the visual direction with
practical considerations such as layout, visitor movement and presentation
needs. Each enquiry starts with the type of event, the available space and the
intended experience, providing a useful basis for agreeing the work and its next
steps.
