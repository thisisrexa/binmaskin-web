Dar Al Hulul is a digital marketplace. It connects products, services, and
customers through discovery that is easier to trust and easier to finish.

## What it connects

- Products and services that need a clear path to the right customer
- A discovery experience that stays understandable in Arabic and English
- A commercial surface that can grow without losing the thread

The marketplace is a place to find the right thing, not a pile of listings.
