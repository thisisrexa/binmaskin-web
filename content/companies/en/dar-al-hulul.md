In development.

Dar Al Hulul is being developed around a simple connection: what one person has,
makes or produces, and what another person needs. Its discovery approach starts
with that need, helping people find relevant products and services through a
clearer, more approachable experience.

The product direction brings search and category browsing together, with Arabic
and English at the centre of the experience. The platform is in development,
with discovery shaped around connecting a request with a useful next step. The
emphasis stays on understanding what people are looking for and making the path
easier to follow.

The marketplace is a place to find the right thing, not a pile of listings.
