In development.

Operith is being developed to connect the work that begins in messages,
meetings, calls and business systems. It brings context, responsibility and
follow-up closer together, helping teams understand what was requested, who owns
the next step, and what evidence is needed to close the loop.

The approach is designed to work alongside existing tools. Tasks, decisions,
commitments and waiting states keep their different meanings, while important
context travels with the work. The focus is practical continuity: less time
reconstructing conversations, clearer responsibility, and timely attention when
something needs a decision.

The point is the operating model, not another dashboard for its own sake.
