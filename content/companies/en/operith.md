Operith is an operations platform. Workflows, information, and automation sit
closer together, so a business spends less time chasing its own process.

## What it is for

- Everyday workflows that should not live in scattered threads
- Automation where a repeated step has a clear owner
- A calmer operating picture for teams that already have enough tools

The point is the operating model, not another dashboard for its own sake.
